(1)使用进化算子对基因组进行采样，系统会评估多种智能体架构；表现最佳的架构会在其基因组被用于生成新架构群体之前，先接受一次批评器梯度优化步骤。
(2)两种G2N实现方案：基于优势智能体批评器（A2c） 的G2Ac，以及基于近端策略优化（ppo） 的G2ppo。

(3)以及采用软更新机制(if 后代个体优于当前RL智能体: 该智能体将通过软更新机制向该更优后代方向进)
   (未突变的智能体也会被纳入进化种群中)

-2.4 Using a Surrogate Fitness
why: (Algorithem Above): 都需要对智能体进行评估以执行进化选择步骤，这可能会降低样本效率
  sc-ERL & pGps
(Using critic network as a surrogate for evaluating evolutionary individuals.)
将批评器作为适应度的替代模型，从而无需生成额外样本即可估算新个体的适应度 [该模块的通用性]
[sc-ERL:Materials/ForA/A surrogate-assisted controller for expensive evolutionary.pdf]
(1)sc-ERL架构具有通用性，可应用于我们迄今提出的任何组合方案。实际应用中，该架构被应用于ERL 、pDERL和cEm-RL，并分别衍生出前两种场景下的sERL和spDERL算法。
(2)pGps算法基于相同理念构建，但将其应用于进化机制与强化学习机制的特定组合框架中
   (In more detail, half of the population is filled with agents
   evaluated from the surrogate fitness, whereas the other half are generated with cem. Furthermore,
   the current td3 actor is injected into the population and benefits from a soft update toward the
   best agent in the population.)

对连续动作空间的优化处理(∵argmax_a Q(s_t,a)的计算复杂)
path1:"在演员-评论家方法中，解决此问题的标准方案是将演员的动作视为最佳解的可靠替代指标"
    即:original: Q(s_t, a_t) ← r(s_t, a_t) +max_a Q(s_(t+1), a) − Q(s_t,a_t)
    => changed:  the estimated best action (¯a_t) = π(s_t)
                 Q(s_t, a_t) ← r(s_t, a_t ) + maxa Q(s_(t+1)), π(s_(t+1)) − Q(s_t, a_t)
path2:a variation-selection method(也就是这一部分的主要介绍内容)
      *一些涉及到的算法大点兵(虽然没有特别细看但是总之先把表格丢在这里说不定能用上呢x):
      -算法-      -Critic update-         -Action Selection-          Policy update
      QT-OPT     CEM(random,64,6,2)     CEM(random,64,6,2)            None
      CGP        CEM(random,64,6,2)     π(s_t)                        BC or DPG
      EAS-RL     PSO(10,10)             π(s_t)                        BC + DPG
      SAC-CEPO   SAC update             CEM(π,60->140,3%->7%,6->14)   BC

    "As Table 2 shows, the qt-opt algorithm [36] simply samples 64 random actions in the action
  space and performs two iterations of cem to get a high-performing action, both for critic updates
  and action selection. It is striking that such a simple method can perform well even in large action
  spaces. This simple idea was then improved in the cgp algorithm [90] so as to avoid the computational cost of action inference. Instead of using cem to sample an action at each timestep, a policy
  network is learned based on the behavior of the cem. This network can be seen as a surrogate of
  the cem sampling process and is trained either from the sampled a¯t using Behavioral Cloning
  (BC) or following a Deterministic Policy Gradient (DPG) step from the critic"
